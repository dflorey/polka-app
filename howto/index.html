Operating manual FS-3

How to use Polka

A band of world leaders on an LED screen, playing a polka at 120 BPM. Switch the acts on and off, throw in effects, shape the sound with the knobs, then record your mix and share it as a link.

01 Quick start

  1. Wait for the screen to light up. The recordings download first; until then the keys don’t respond.
  2. Switch an act on with the keys at the bottom (or 1–6). Your first tap or key press also turns the sound on.
  3. Add the others. Each comes in on the next beat, in step with the rest.
  4. Play with the effects and knobs, then press Rec to record a take and Share it.

02 The band

Six keys, each with an LED in its act’s colour, switch the acts on and off. An act that is off stands dimmed in grey; switched on, it plays and its flag is hoisted on the pole at the side of the stage.

1 USA
Trump on the bass drum, on every beat
2 China
Xi on double bass
3 Russia
Putin singing through a megaphone
4 EU
von der Leyen on recorder
5 Germany
Merz on guitar
6 Choir
Merkel, Orbán and Modi

The music loops are eight bars long and always come in where the song has got to, so they stay in step; the voice starts from the top. Every start is a fresh one: all acts and effects off, the knobs at their defaults.

03 Effect keys

Five keys above the band keys, each with its own LED colour. The screen reacts to every one.

Repeat

Waits for the next beat, then stutters its first sixteenth note for two beats. The picture glitches meanwhile.

Riser

A two-second white-noise build-up, with fireworks over the band and the sunburst spinning up in rainbow colours.

Siren

A dub siren, with red and blue beacons sweeping the stage and the colours cycling.

Vinyl

On and off (its LED stays lit): the mix sounds like a worn 78 on a gramophone, and the picture turns into an old black-and-white film.

Pause

Hold it down: a tape stop slows the sound to a halt and the picture fades to dark. Let go and the tape spins back up into the beat. The band carries on silently meanwhile.

04 Knobs

Drag up or down (hold Shift for fine steps), scroll, or use the arrow keys when selected. Double-click a knob to reset it. The value shows below each knob.

Mega

The megaphone on Putin’s voice only: clean at 0 %, a distorted bullhorn at 100 % (default 25 %).

Drive

Saturation, then warm tube drive from half way, then buzzing fuzz in the last quarter, without getting louder.

Flanger

A slow jet-plane sweep over the whole band.

Filter

A DJ filter: off in the centre, a resonant low-pass sweep to the left, a high-pass sweep to the right.

Delay

Stereo echoes: eighth notes on the left, dotted eighths on the right, louder and longer as you turn it up.

Reverb

Puts the band in a concert hall, 0–100 %.

05 Record, play & share

A small transport hangs into the top of the screen: three keys and a readout with the time or what just happened.

Rec

Records a take (R): how everything is set at the start, then every key, knob and effect you touch, for up to ten minutes. With the band silent it only arms (“Rec ready”, the LED blinks) and the take starts with the first act you switch on. Press again to end it.

Play

Plays the take back from its start (P). Touch any control and you take over from there. While recording it is Pause / Resume: set things up anew without it being recorded, and it all takes effect at the moment you paused.

Share

Stores the take online and shares its link: on a computer it is copied (“Link copied”), on phones and tablets the share sheet opens. Whoever opens the link hears your take as soon as the band has loaded, or after a tap where the browser wants one (“Tap to play”).

07 Questions

The band moves but I hear nothing

Browsers only allow sound after you tap or press a key on the page. Your first tap turns it on; until then the band plays silently. Check your volume, and on iPhone the silent switch.

The screen stays dark

The recordings (about 12 MB) are still downloading. Once they have all arrived the picture lights up and the band starts. On a slow connection this takes a moment, only the first time.

Which settings are kept?

None: every start is a fresh one, with all acts off and the knobs at their defaults. To keep a mix, record a take and share it.

Can I use it offline, like an app?

Yes. Install it from your browser (“Add to Home Screen” on iPhone and iPad, “Install app” in Chrome and Edge). It keeps the recordings, so it then works without a connection; sharing takes needs one.
